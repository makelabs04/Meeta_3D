ABOUT THE PLATFORM

Meet MEETA.

Designed by MAKE Labs Research and Development, MEETA is an interactive humanoid robot concept for welcoming visitors, sharing information and demonstrating human–robot interaction.

Explore each configuration in 3D, move around the model from any angle and try its interactive gestures.

01 / WELCOME

Guest Welcoming MEETA

A reception-style configuration for greeting visitors and introducing an experience.

02 / GUIDE

Guiding MEETA

A tablet-equipped configuration for presenting guidance and information.

03 / EXPLORE

MEETA

Explore the humanoid form, its articulated arms and its interactive presentation gestures.
